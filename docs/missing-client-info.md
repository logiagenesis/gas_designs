# Missing Client Information

**Everything on this list is currently hidden from the website.** Nothing here
has been guessed, inferred or filled in with a plausible-sounding placeholder.

Each item is gated in `src/lib/site-config.ts` under `UNCONFIRMED`. A fact only
appears on the site once its `confirmed` flag is flipped to `true` and a real
value is supplied — the components read the gate, never a literal, so an
unconfirmed fact is structurally incapable of reaching a page.

---

## 1. PRIORITY — the phone number

A candidate number exists:

> **+27 61 039 7034**

**It is not published, and it must not be published until Pierre confirms
ownership and exclusive use.** The brief records that this number is currently
shared with DP Energies. Publishing a number that routes to, or is also
advertised by, another gas company creates three concrete problems:

1. Enquiries generated by Gas Designs marketing may be answered by someone else.
2. Google may merge or conflate the two businesses' listings, because a shared
   phone number is one of the strongest entity-matching signals there is.
3. If the relationship between the businesses changes, every place the number has
   been published — site, listings, directories, printed material — has to be
   corrected at once.

**This number is deliberately not written into the application source.** Because
`site-config.ts` is imported by client components, any literal there would be
compiled into the public JavaScript bundle even if it were never rendered. It is
recorded here, in documentation, and nowhere else.

**To publish it once confirmed:**

1. Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_BUSINESS_PHONE` in the deployment environment.
2. In `src/lib/site-config.ts`, change `phone` from `UNSET` to
   `{ value: process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_BUSINESS_PHONE ?? null, confirmed: true }`.
3. Nothing else changes. The header call link, the footer, the contact page and
   the `ContactPoint` structured data all appear automatically.

**Questions for Pierre:**

- Does Gas Designs own this number outright, or is it shared?
- If shared, is a separate number being obtained?
- Is it the right number for emergency callouts, or should a second line be used?
- Should WhatsApp be enabled on it?

**The words "licensed", "registered", "accredited", "certified" and "insured" do
not appear anywhere on this site.** On a gas safety site those words are
load-bearing: a customer relies on them when deciding whether an installation is
legal, and an insurer relies on them when deciding whether to pay a claim. They
are not marketing adjectives and they stay out until there is a document behind
each one.

**Questions for Pierre:**

- What is the SAQCC Gas registration number, and in whose name is it held?
- Which categories does that registration cover (domestic, commercial,
  industrial, bulk)?
- Is Gas Designs an LPGSA member?
- Is there public liability cover, and what is the sum insured?

## 3. Location and availability

| Item | Status | Consequence while missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical / trading address | Not supplied | No `LocalBusiness` schema, no map, no address in the footer |
| Service area or coverage radius | Not supplied | No town, suburb or province is named anywhere on the site |
| Opening hours | Not supplied | No hours published, no `openingHours` schema |
| After-hours / emergency availability | Not supplied | **No "24/7" claim anywhere** |
| Response time | Not supplied | No response-time promise anywhere |

**This is the largest single SEO cost of the unconfirmed list.** Gas installation
is a local-intent search category: people search "gas installer *near me*" and
"gas COC *Pretoria*". Without a confirmed area, the site cannot:

- emit `LocalBusiness` or `ContactPoint` structured data (the brief bans empty
  NAP schema, and empty NAP is worse than none — it tells Google the entity has
  no location);
- rank for any geographic modifier;
- appear in the local map pack;
- be submitted to a Google Business Profile.

Everything else on the site is built to be genuinely competitive. This one gap is
what holds it back.

**Questions for Pierre:**

- Which city or region is the base?
- How far out do you travel, and is there a call-out charge beyond a radius?
- What are the real working hours?
- Is there genuine after-hours emergency cover? If yes, on what number and at
  what hours — "24/7" is only publishable if it is literally true.

Real photography would be the single highest-value addition to this site. What is
worth capturing, in rough order of value:

1. A finished commercial kitchen manifold — labelled isolation, tidy pipe runs.
2. A bulk tank installation showing the base, the restraint and the separation.
3. A domestic cylinder housing done properly.
4. A pressure test in progress, gauge readable.
5. Before and after of a remedial job.

Shoot in daylight, landscape, no people's faces without permission, and get the
client's consent to publish each site.

## 7. Service scope — detail to confirm

The nine service categories themselves are confirmed by the brief. These are
details *within* them that a reader might reasonably infer, and that have been
written generally rather than specifically:

**Across all services**
- Supply-and-install, or install-only?
- LPG only, or natural gas reticulation as well?
- Any workmanship warranty, and for how long?

**Certificates of Compliance**
- Who signs, under which registration?
- Are certificates issued for installations carried out by others?
- Which SANS standards do you want cited by name? The site currently refers to
  "the applicable SANS standards" and to the Pressure Equipment Regulations under
  the Occupational Health and Safety Act, without naming a specific number,
  because the correct citation has not been confirmed.

**Bulk LPG**
- Maximum tank capacity installed?
- Are tanks supplied, or installed on a supplier's behalf?
- Is gas supply and refilling arranged?

**Industrial**
- Which sectors and plant types have actually been worked on?
- Is shutdown and turnaround work undertaken?

**Basic Electrical**
- Where exactly does your scope end and a registered electrician's begin?
- Can an electrical CoC be issued? *(Not claimed on the site.)*

**Leak detection and emergency repairs**
- What is the real callout availability?
- Is there a response time you are willing to publish?
