Compliance

Every installation is tested and certified.

All work is carried out by a registered gas installer and pressure tested before handover. Where required, you receive a Certificate of Conformity and the installation records for your insurer, landlord or body corporate.

  • Supply sized for the equipment actually connected.
  • Shut-off valves fitted where they can be reached in an emergency.
  • Pressure and leak testing completed before handover.
  • Certificate and documentation issued for your records.
