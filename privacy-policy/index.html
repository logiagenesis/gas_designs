Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information this website collects, why it is collected, and what happens to it.

This policy has not been reviewed by a legal practitioner. It should be checked and adopted by Gas Designs before the site goes live. Company registration details: [CLIENT TO CONFIRM].

What we collect

The enquiry form asks for your name, phone number, email address, suburb or area, property type, the service you need, and your message. The form also records the page you submitted from and the time.

The form includes a hidden field used to catch automated submissions. A person never sees it or fills it in.

Why we collect it

We use these details to answer your enquiry and to quote for the work you describe. We do not sell them, rent them, or add you to a marketing list.

Consent

The form asks you to confirm that we may use your details to reply. You do not have to use the form. You are welcome to email us directly at pierre@gasdesigns.co.za instead.

How it reaches us

Submissions are sent to our business email address. They are not stored in a database on this website.

How long we keep it

Enquiries stay in our email for as long as we need them for the work and our records. Ask us and we will delete yours, unless we must keep it for legal or accounting reasons.

Who else sees it

Our email provider handles the message in the course of delivering it. Our hosting company processes the request that carries your submission. We share enquiry details with nobody else unless you ask us to, or the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site can load Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics. Where these are enabled they may set cookies and record which pages were viewed. What you type into the enquiry form is never sent to any analytics service.

If no analytics container is configured, this website sets no cookies of its own.

Your rights

The Protection of Personal Information Act gives you rights over information about you. You may ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, and object to how we use it.

To exercise any of these, email pierre@gasdesigns.co.za. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Security

Submissions travel over an encrypted connection. Mail credentials are held on the server and are never exposed to your browser. No website can promise perfect security, and this one does not.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to pierre@gasdesigns.co.za.
